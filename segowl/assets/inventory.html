Inventory

Click a cell to edit. Drag to select a block, Ctrl+click or Ctrl+drag to add blocks. With cells selected, just type: the value fills every selected cell. Drag the handle down to extend a series (vm-01 → vm-02…). Ctrl+D copies the top cell down. Ctrl+V pastes a block from a spreadsheet (rows are added as needed). Changes save automatically.
