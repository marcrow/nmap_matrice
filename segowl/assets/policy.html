Flow Policy

Rows = from, columns = to. Click a cell (or Enter, arrows to move) to decide what may cross. ● = ports observed open in the Scans, so you decide on facts. Empty = undeclared: shown apart in the report, never a Finding. The most specific rule wins (Machine > Zone > *), deny wins ties. Changes save automatically.
Shift+click selects a rectangle, Ctrl+click picks scattered cells, a Zone header its row or column; Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy a decision to them, Del empties them, Ctrl+Z undoes.
