Auditor Kits

A kit carries your Port Groups, Scan Profiles and Port Labels from Mission to Mission: only port_groups.csv, scan_profiles.csv and port_labels.csv, never client data. Applying a kit shows a row-by-row diff with the Mission; you pick which of its files replace the Mission's, the others stay as they are.
